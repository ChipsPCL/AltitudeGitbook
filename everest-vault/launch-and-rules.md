🏔️ EVEREST — LAUNCH & CONTRACT RULES
Launch Date: TBA!
To make sure everyone has enough time to get their ALTITUDE staked before rewards begin, Everest will open with a 24–48 hour pre-launch staking window.
During this window, the UI will be live and users will be able to deposit ALTITUDE into Everest, but no rewards will be distributed yet. This gives people who may not be available at the exact launch time a fair opportunity to get positioned beforehand.
Minimum Staking Balance: 5,000 ALTITUDE after fees
The deposit fee is 1%, so your net staked balance must be at least 5,000 ALTITUDE.
Once your position is above the minimum, you can deposit any additional amount.
Partial withdrawals are allowed as long as your remaining staked balance does not fall below 5,000 ALTITUDE.
Example:
If your staked balance is 5,100 ALTITUDE:
✅ Withdraw 100 ALT → allowed
❌ Withdraw 101 ALT → transaction reverts
✅ Withdraw all 5,100 ALT → allowed
All withdrawals carry a 2% withdrawal fee.
🏔️ When the pre-launch window ends, the Everest reward reserve will be funded and ALTITUDE rewards will begin accruing for stakers. No claim fees! Buy/Sell taxes apply!
There is also an Emergency Withdraw function built into the contract.
If the website/UI were ever unavailable, users can access this and other functions directly through BaseScan. Emergency Withdraw closes the entire staking position, applies the normal 2% withdrawal fee, and forfeits any pending rewards.
